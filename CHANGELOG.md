# Changelog

## [0.2.1] - 2026-10-21
- The burglar's eye mask stands out: the beanie is now navy and sits a row higher, with a strip of forehead between it and the mask.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-21
- Criminals to put in your prison, on both editions: a striped convict, an inmate in an orange jumpsuit, a masked burglar and a gangster in a pinstripe suit. They shuffle about, keep away from guard dogs and hit back when hit. Guards leave them alone. Spawn them with the Criminal spawn egg.
- The prison cell blueprint now comes with a criminal inside.

## [0.1.3] - 2026-10-20
- Bedrock: sneak and use a camera with anything but the linker in your hand to start or stop its panning, matching the button shown on touch screens.
- The Bedrock self test keeps its test animals still, so the laser and motion sensor checks no longer fail at random.

## [0.1.2] - 2026-10-20
- Bedrock: security cameras can now be interacted with, so the Camera Linker can store them, and sneaking with an empty hand starts or stops the panning. On touch screens, a button shows up when you hold the linker or sneak.

## [0.1.1] - 2026-10-20
- Java: the Camera Linker now links cameras when you use it on a monitor. Before, the monitor ignored the linker and tried to show its (missing) cameras instead.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19
First release, for Bedrock and Java (Fabric):
- Security cameras that pan from side to side, a Camera Linker, and monitors. On Java the monitor shows the camera's live picture; on both editions you can look through the camera.
- Laser tripwires, laser fences and laser receivers. Motion sensors, alarm sirens and searchlights that light up where they point.
- Keycard readers with Guard, Officer and Warden keycards, and barred cell doors.
- Prison walls, reinforced concrete, prison bars, barbed wire and razor wire.
- Pit trapdoors disguised as stone bricks.
- Timed bombs (5 to 60 second fuse, defuse with shears) and tripwire bombs.
- Bridge winches that wind a rope bridge out and back in.
- Blueprints for a guard tower, a prison cell and a gatehouse.
- Security guards, guard dogs to tame, and the baton.
- English and Danish.
